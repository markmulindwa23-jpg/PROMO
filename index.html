Create an account, choose Post an ad, pick how long it should run, then add your details and photos.

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

PROMO-HUB operates this site from Uganda. Contact us at promohub256@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your name, username, recovery email and a hashed password, plus the details you put in ads (including phone number and photos) and simple counts of ad views and contacts.

How we use it

To run your account, show your ads to buyers and show you how your ads perform.

Who can see it

Ad details, including the phone number, are public. Your password is never shown.

Payments

Online payment is not connected in this version of the site, so no payment details are collected.

Cookies and device storage

We store small items in your browser to keep you logged in, remember recently viewed ads and limit repeat enquiries. We do not use advertising trackers.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in your own browser on this device and is not sent to any server. You can request deletion of your account and data from My profile under Delete account. An administrator reviews the request. You can also contact us.

Questions

What can I advertise?

Products, services, property, vehicles, events and jobs. Misleading or illegal ads are removed.

Can I change my ad after posting?

Yes. Open My profile, choose Edit, and update the details or photos. Editing is free.

What happens when my ad ends?

It leaves the page automatically. Post a new ad whenever you want to run again.

How do I report a bad ad?

Open the ad and tap Report ad. We review every report.
